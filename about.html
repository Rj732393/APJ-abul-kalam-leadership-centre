Who We Are

A centre built on one belief: leadership is a practice, not a title.

The Dr. A P J Abdul Kalam Centre For Leadership And Social Change works with young Indians to turn civic conviction into civic capability — grounded in Dr. Kalam's own life as scientist, teacher, and President.

Our Story

The Centre was founded in 2018 by a group of educators and former civil servants who noticed the same gap on every campus they visited: students who cared deeply about their country but had never been taught how its institutions actually worked, or how to stand for something within them.

We took Dr. Kalam's own trajectory — from a boat-owner's son in Rameswaram to India's "People's President" — as our working model. Not because it can be repeated exactly, but because it proves what disciplined, value-based effort can do over a lifetime.

Since then, the Centre has run leadership fellowships, campus voter drives, and community action labs across twelve states, working directly with colleges, district administrations, and first-time community organisers.

Vision & Mission

Vision. To nurture value-based leadership by encouraging young people to participate in the electoral process, and to build just and equitable societies from the ground up.

Mission. To facilitate personal transformation through education that is relevant, rigorous, and rooted in service — equipping young leaders with both the conviction and the competence to act.

What We Value

Four commitments run through every programme we design.

01

Service before self

Every fellow works on a real community problem before they graduate — leadership proven in practice, not certificates.

02

Discipline over inspiration

Motivation fades; habits don't. Our programmes build repeatable civic habits — voting, questioning, organising.

03

Institutions over individuals

We build lasting partnerships with colleges and civic bodies so the work continues after a fellow graduates.

04

Access over exclusivity

Free for participants, prioritising first-generation college students and smaller-town institutions.

Nurturing value-based leadership and civic participation among India's young people.
