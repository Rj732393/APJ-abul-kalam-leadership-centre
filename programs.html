What We Run

Three programmes. One trajectory — from citizen to leader.

Each track builds on the last, moving a young person from awareness, to mentored practice, to independent community action.

STAGE 01

Civic Foundations

A semester-long campus workshop series covering the Constitution, the structure of local and state government, and the electoral process — ending with on-campus voter registration drives. Open to any first- or second-year student; no prior civics background required.

STAGE 02

Leadership Fellowship

A year-long, selective cohort programme. Fellows are paired one-on-one with mentors from public service, science, and local government, and work in small teams on a real community problem chosen with a partner institution. Includes monthly in-person sessions and a mid-year residential.

STAGE 03

Community Action Labs

Graduating fellows return to their own districts with a small seed grant to run voter awareness drives, civic audits, or local initiatives of their own design — with continued mentor check-ins for one full year after launch.

Who Can Apply

Civic Foundations is open to any enrolled undergraduate at a partner institution — no application required, just show up.

The Leadership Fellowship is selective and runs one cohort per year, opening applications each March. Priority is given to first-generation college students and applicants from smaller towns and districts.

Community Action Labs are only open to fellows who have completed the Leadership Fellowship, ensuring every project has a trained lead behind it.
